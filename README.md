# Privacy Policy for Project Frenzy

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

Project Frenzy respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Project Frenzy mobile game.

## Information We Collect

Project Frenzy does not currently collect, transmit, or store personal or sensitive user information.

The current version of the game does not require user accounts, social-media login, location information, camera or microphone access, advertising identifiers, analytics services, or personal profile information.

## Gameplay Data

Game progress, settings, and similar gameplay information may be stored locally on your device where applicable. This information is not transmitted to the developer.

Project Frenzy does not currently operate an online leaderboard or require players to submit names or other identifying information.

## Advertising

Project Frenzy does not currently display third-party advertisements or use advertising SDKs.

## Analytics and Crash Reporting

Project Frenzy does not currently use third-party analytics or crash-reporting services.

## Purchases

The current version of Project Frenzy does not contain in-app purchases.

A future version may offer a one-time purchase to unlock additional game content. Purchases made through Google Play will be processed by Google Play. Project Frenzy does not collect or store your payment card or banking information.

This Privacy Policy will be updated before any material change to the way the game collects or handles user information.

## Sharing of Information

Because Project Frenzy does not currently collect personal or sensitive user information, the developer does not sell, rent, or share such information with third parties.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Project Frenzy does not currently maintain developer-controlled user accounts or store personal user data on external servers.

Game data stored locally on your device may generally be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the game.

## Children's Privacy

Project Frenzy does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Security

Because Project Frenzy does not currently transmit or store personal user information on developer-controlled servers, no personal user database is maintained by the developer.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Project Frenzy gains new features or services. Any updated policy will reflect the data practices of the version of the game then available.
